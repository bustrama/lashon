Train your own wake word for Lashon

Want Lashon to listen for a custom phrase — "Hey Lashon", your own name, anything? Train a small classifier in Google Colab. About 30–60 minutes, free, all in your browser. The result runs locally on your machine.

Don't want to train one? Lashon's Settings Hub → Wake word → More wake words offers one-click installs of "Hey Jarvis", "Alexa", "Hey Mycroft", and "Hey Rhasspy". They're released under CC-BY-NC-4.0 (non-commercial use only) and Lashon shows the licence before installing. Models you train yourself have no such restriction.

Step by step

  1. Open the training notebook.

    Open in Google Colab

    The notebook is still being polished — if a cell errors, the message usually points at the line to tweak.

  2. Switch to a GPU runtime. Colab menu → Runtime → Change runtime type → T4 GPU → Save. Without a GPU this takes hours; with the free T4 it's ~30–60 minutes.
  3. Set your phrase. Near the top of the notebook, change target_word = "hey lashon" to whatever you want. Hebrew works too. Pick something distinctive and 2–3 syllables — single common words like "hello" or "yes" will fire constantly.
  4. Run all cells. Runtime → Run all, then wait. The notebook synthesises hundreds of recordings of your phrase, mixes in background noise, downloads a precomputed set of "not your phrase" audio (~30 GB on Colab's disk), trains a small classifier, and exports it as ONNX. The dataset download is the slowest cell.
  5. Download the .onnx file. When training finishes the notebook produces a file named after your phrase, e.g. hey_lashon.onnx. In Colab's left Files panel, right-click and choose Download.
  6. Drop it into Lashon's wake-words folder:
    OSPath
    Windows%LOCALAPPDATA%\dev.lashon.desktop\models\wakewords\
    macOS~/Library/Application Support/dev.lashon.desktop/models/wakewords/
    Linux~/.local/share/dev.lashon.desktop/models/wakewords/
  7. Pick it in the Hub. Double-click the tongue → Settings → Wake word → toggle Enable on, then select your phrase from the picker. The wake worker live-reloads in under a second. Say your phrase — dictation opens.

If the picker doesn't show your model — the file must be directly in wakewords/ (not a subfolder) and end in .onnx. The picker turns the filename stem into a friendly name automatically: my_dragon.onnx → My Dragon.

If it shows but never fires — confirm Enable is on and you're saying the exact phrase you trained. Slide sensitivity up if needed.
