Ottid

אוטיד

A local voice assistant for dictation in Hebrew and English — specially tuned and corrected for Hebrew. Injects text into any app, all on your computer, no cloud.

The name is ditto spelled backwards: the English word for “the same again”. To a Hebrew ear it also echoes עתיד, “future”.
